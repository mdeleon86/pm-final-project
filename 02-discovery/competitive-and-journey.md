# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

## Persona
- **Role:** Frontline delivery drivers using RouteLogic Velocity
- **Goal:** Complete assigned delivery routes quickly and accurately, with reliable access to stops and delivery information.
- **Friction:** App crashes, unreliable offline access, and buried core actions interrupt deliveries and force drivers to use paper manifests or call dispatch.

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** Paper delivery manifests, screenshots of route information, and phone calls to the dispatch office.
- **The process:** 1. Save screenshots of the assigned route or carry a paper manifest as backup.
2. Begin deliveries using RouteLogic.
3. When the app crashes or loses connectivity, consult the saved route information.
4. If the remaining stops are unavailable, call dispatch to retrieve them.
5. Continue deliveries using the recovered information.
- **Core frustration:** Drivers cannot rely on RouteLogic to maintain access to their assigned stops. When the app crashes or loses connectivity, they must interrupt deliveries and rely on manual backups or dispatch assistance, causing delays and frustration.
- **The evidence:** "The app crashed mid-route and lost my remaining stops. I had to call the office to read them to me off a screen. Twenty minutes gone." (UXR-03)

Five of seven drivers also reported carrying paper manifests in case the app fails (UXR-12).

## Future-state journey map
1. Start Route | Driver opens assigned route with offline access → Confident route information remains available → Eliminates paper backups
2. Navigate Stops | Driver accesses next stop through a simplified interface → Focused on deliveries → Reduces unnecessary navigation.
3. Complete Delivery | Driver records delivery and receives confirmation → Confident information is saved → Eliminates repeated attempts.
4. Finish Route | Driver completes route and synchronizes records → Confident deliveries are recorded → Eliminates manual data recovery.

## Competitive advantages
Advantage #1: Reliability- Reliable offline route access eliminates dependence on paper manifests, screenshots, and calls to dispatch.

Advantage #2: Speed- Simplified navigation makes essential delivery actions faster and easier than navigating complex menus or using manual workarounds.

Advantage #3: Trust- Clear delivery confirmations and automatic synchronization reduce uncertainty and help drivers trust their delivery records.
