# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Frontline delivery driver who completes multiple stops daily using RouteLogic Velocity.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Complete deliveries quickly and accurately while staying on schedule using one reliable platform.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** Essential actions are buried in complex menus, requiring unnecessary taps and slowing down routine deliveries. Drivers resort to texting dispatchers and keeping paper backups.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Text messaging and phone communication with dispatchers instead of completing delivery updates directly in RouteLogic.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Driver completes a delivery.
2. Driver skips RouteLogic's three-screen "Mark Delivered" process.
3. Driver texts the dispatcher that the stop is complete.
4. Dispatcher receives the update and may need to update RouteLogic manually (inferred).
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** Marking a delivery complete requires too many steps, making a routine action unnecessarily slow. Drivers resort to texting dispatchers, creating extra coordination and potentially outdated delivery records.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** UXR-01: A driver reported "started just texting my dispatcher instead."

BUG-2055: Mark Delivered requires three taps across three screens.

UXR-09: Dispatchers report completed stops remaining marked "in progress."
